Position

One sentence, then the jobs

Free tools for Ultimate Football Manager 27 that do the counting so you can do the managing.

UFMWiz is for the player who already likes UFM and wants to spend less time doing arithmetic on a phone: which cards finish a PTC, whether a squad clears Chemistry thresholds, what a pack actually holds, how many wins the Weekend League rank needs. No UFM companion site existed when this book was written, so the job is also to be the reference people link to.

What UFMWiz is not

  • Not official. Not made, endorsed or reviewed by Gamebasics or Miniclip.
  • Not a hack. UFM's YouTube results are full of "unlimited gems" scams. We never offer mods, generators, account services or anything that touches the game client. Saying so plainly is part of the brand.
  • Not a pack hype channel. We report odds and value. We don't celebrate pulls or push spending.

Voice

The teammate who has already done the maths

UFMWiz sounds like a player who knows the rules cold and tells you the answer first. Short sentences, real numbers, the game's own words. Confident because it is checked, never because it is loud.

Answer first

Lead with the number or the decision, then the reason. "You need 14 wins out of 15" before any explanation of points.

Use the game's words

Players search for what the game prints. PTC, not SBC. Focus, not form. Quick Match, not friendly. See Words.

Show the working

When a number decides something, show how it was reached in a line or a chalk figure. The reader should be able to check us.

Honest about the meter

Energy, fitness and pack odds cost players real money. Say what something costs and what it returns, without spin in either direction.

Never "boss"

The game's coach character calls the player "boss". That is the game's voice. Ours addresses the reader as "you".

Season-aware

Everything in UFM resets in summer. Date any claim that can change, and say when advice expires.

Tiers, qualities and rarities

Tiers: Amateur 1–3, Semi-Pro 1–3, Pro 1–3, Legendary 1–3. Qualities: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Gold Rare, Elite, Premium. Rarities take the game's display name, with the short form allowed in tables: Team of the Week (TOTW), Player of the Month (POTM), Team of the Year (TOTY), Team of the Season (TOTS), Screamers, Most Wanted, Transfer Stars and so on. Write the rarity name in full the first time on any page.

The game's own text is inconsistent in places. The Halloween rarity displays as "Matchday", and "Kick-Off" displays as "European Evolutions". Always write what the player sees on the card, not the internal name.

Interface copy

  • Headers are the game's pattern: a one-to-three-word title set in caps, and a gold sentence beside it that says what the screen is for. "Market · Search the market and strengthen your team".
  • Buttons are a verb, two words at most: Complete, Add card, Clear all, Apply filters. The result names what happened: "Challenge saved".
  • Errors say what went wrong and how to fix it: "That lineup has 10 players. Add one more to check Chemistry."
  • Empty states on a full page are chalk with one line of instruction. Inside a working panel they are one muted sentence.
  • Reported, not verified. When a figure comes from players rather than the game (energy regen time, pack prices), say "players report" and date it.

Rules that protect the site

  • No game files on the site. No sprites, card art, pack art, currency icons, fonts or data copied out of the app. Values that describe the look are fine; the files themselves are not.
  • No data taken from the game's servers. Card data comes from what players can see and submit, entered by hand or imported from CSV, as on FHCWiz.
  • No logos or likenesses. No UFM, Gamebasics or Miniclip marks. No player photos. Club crests are drawn as plain two-colour shields.
  • No account services. No selling, boosting or sharing accounts. UFM's own terms ban account transfers.
  • No monetised pack hype. If the site ever carries ads or affiliate links, they never sit beside pack odds.
