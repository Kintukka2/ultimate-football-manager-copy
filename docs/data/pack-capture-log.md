# Pack capture log

The working record of the pack odds screens behind the Packs page. Every pack
in Ultimate Football Manager 27 has an info button that shows "the odds for at
least 1 player in this pack", by rating band and by rarity. Those numbers are
what a player can see, so they are what we record: copied off a screenshot of
that screen, a row per pack.

No pack is listed until someone has sent its screen. Nothing here is taken
from the game's files, and nothing is estimated from pulls: a pull is luck,
the screen is the rule.

## The rules

- **Copy the screen, exactly.** The pack's name as the game writes it, its
  player count, and every line of both panels, with the percentages as
  printed (`2.5%`, not `2.50%` or `1 in 40`).
- **Date it.** Odds can change between events. A row is the screen on the day
  it was captured, and a later capture of the same pack is a new row, not an
  edit.
- **Leave the top-players list out.** The screen's "Possible Top 100 Players in
  this Pack" is the top of a larger pool that changes as cards are released,
  so it isn't tracked.
- **Where it comes from, when known.** The shop, a PTC reward, a Weekend League
  rank, a mission, an event shop. Blank means not known yet, not none.
- **Credit by handle**, spelled their way.

## The record

One block per pack, in this shape:

```
Pack:        Example Pack
Players:     5
Price:       1,000 coins          (as the shop shows it, if it does)
Captured:    2026-10-30 by TesterOne (Discord #packs)
Comes from:  Pack store
Ratings:     75+ 100% · 80+ 42% · 85+ 6.1% · 90+ 0.4%
Rarities:    Normal 100% · Team of the Week 1.2%
Also shown:  Unsellable · Guaranteed
```

That example is made up, to show the shape only.
